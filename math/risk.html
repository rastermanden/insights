Instructions

Enter the number of events and total subjects in both control and treatment groups.

Use the arrows to adjust values or type directly; results update automatically.

Example: If 30/100 controls and 20/100 treated had an event, enter 30, 100, 20, 100.

To reset, type default values (e.g., 162, 21728, 8, 21720).

Harm–Benefit: SAE rate defaults to 1 in 800 from Fraiman et al. (2022) reanalysis of mRNA COVID‑19 vaccine trials — PubMed 36055877. Hospitalization rate defaults to 1 in 10,000 (background risk without vaccine). The calculator multiplies that rate by the trial-derived VE to estimate prevented hospitalizations per vaccinated person, then divides the SAE rate by it.
