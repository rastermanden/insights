Instructions

Enter event counts and totals for control and treatment groups for both the primary endpoint and serious adverse events (SAE). Results update automatically.

Default values (162 / 21,728 control, 8 / 21,720 treatment for the primary endpoint; 33 / 60 SAE) come from the Pfizer–BioNTech BNT162b2 phase 3 trial — Polack et al. NEJM 2020 (NEJMoa2034577) — combined with the SAE arm counts from Fraiman et al. (2022) reanalysis (PubMed 36055877).

Relative vs absolute. Relative measures (RR, RRR, RRI) describe how risk changes proportionally between groups — they ignore how common the event is. Absolute measures (ARR, ARI, NNT, NNH) describe how risk changes for an individual in real terms. The two can diverge dramatically when the baseline rate is small: a 95% relative risk reduction may correspond to a <1% absolute risk reduction.

NNT = number of people who must be treated for one to benefit. NNH = number who must be treated for one to be harmed (here, suffer an SAE). Both are derived from absolute differences.

SAE = serious adverse event: fatal, life-threatening, requires/prolongs hospitalization, causes persistent disability, or congenital anomaly.
