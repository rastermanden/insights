What's going on?

Each group on its own shows a negative trend — within Group A, B, or C, larger x tends to mean smaller y.

But ignore the groups and fit a single line to all points and the line slopes upward. The relationship reverses sign.

This is Simpson's paradox: a hidden grouping variable confounds the overall picture. Drag Confounding strength to 0 % to collapse the groups onto each other and watch the paradox vanish — the overall fit then matches the within-group slope.

Real-world flavour: imagine x = hours of weekly exercise and y = cholesterol level, with the groups being age brackets. Within each bracket more exercise lowers cholesterol, but older people exercise more and also have higher cholesterol — so the lurking variable (age) flips the apparent direction.
